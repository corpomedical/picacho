## First comment (the maker story — post immediately after launch)

> Hi Product Hunt — solo founder here.
>
> I built Picacho because every AI video tool I tried had the same quiet
> problem: the character in shot two was almost — but not quite — the person
> from shot one. "Almost" is exactly what audiences notice, and no tool
> would even tell me when it happened.
>
> So Picacho measures it. You set up a character once — a photo, a few
> traits, optional brand rules. Every generation runs a full pipeline: one
> model drafts your prompt, a second reviews it against the character's
> rulebook, then the result is generated and a vision model scores it
> against the identity photo. The number is printed under every render.
> If a generation fails, it retries automatically — and failures never use
> your credits.
>
> Honest notes: it's AI, it can miss; that's why the score exists. Every
> account gets one free generation a day (no card), so you can test whether
> the consistency holds over several days — which is the whole claim.
>
> I'll be here all day. Tell me what breaks, what's confusing, and what
> you'd need before trusting it with a real brand character.

## Launch-day rules (Product Hunt's and common sense)

- **Never** ask anyone to upvote (PH penalizes it; ask people to "check it
  out and leave honest feedback" instead). Share the plain URL, not a
  referral-tracking one.
- Reply to **every** comment within the day, including critical ones —
  visible maker responsiveness converts better than the listing itself.
- Have the reviewer/demo flow rock-solid that day; expect signup spikes and
  a few people poking at edge cases publicly.
- Post one launch-day update comment if something notable happens ("we just
  crossed X signups — most requested feature so far is Y, noted").
